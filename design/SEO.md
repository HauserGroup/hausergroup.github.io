# Search discoverability

A practical catalogue of actions to help researchers, prospective students,
collaborators, funders and AI search tools find the Hauser Group at
<https://hausergroup.org>.

## Ground rules

- **Static, privacy-first, no analytics scripts.** Do not add Google Analytics,
  tracking pixels or cookie banners. Search measurement comes from Google Search
  Console and Bing Webmaster Tools (verified via DNS in Cloudflare).
- **Generated, not hand-maintained.** `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, canonical
  links, Open Graph tags, Schema.org JSON-LD and `llms.txt` are generated by
  Astro at build time from `src/data/*.ts` and verified in CI by
  `npm run check:seo`.
- **Verifiable academic facts only.** Publications link to DOIs, news items link
  to institutional sources, and statistics match published work.
- **Keep lab SEO and software SEO separated.** Product-specific discovery
  (`.pzfx`/`.prism` pages, alternative directories, bio.tools, MCP registries)
  belongs on `curvebook.org` and `scikit-opls`, while `hausergroup.org` owns the
  lab identity, people, research, publications and recruitment.
- **No gaming.** No keyword stuffing, hidden text, doorway pages or orphaned
  near-duplicate pages.

| Find the lab or PI         | "Hauser Group Copenhagen", "Alexander Sebastian Hauser", "pharmacoinformatics KU" | `/`, `/about/`, `/people/alexander-hauser/`, `/contact/` |
| Find a group member        | "<member name> UCPH", "<member name> Hauser group"                                | `/people/[slug]/`, `/people/`, `/alumni/`                |
| Research topics & methods  | "GPCR pharmacogenomics", "human genetic variation GPCR drug response"             | `/`, `/about/`, `/publications/`                         |
| Join the lab / collaborate | "GPCR PhD Copenhagen", "computational pharmacology postdoc Denmark"               | `/opportunities/`, `/services/`, `/contact/`             |
| Find group software        | "Hauser group software", "scikit-opls", "CurveBook Hauser"                        | `/software/`                                             |
| Check papers & awards      | "34% approved drugs GPCR Hauser", "DARK-PEP ERC", "NeuroPepX Lundbeck"            | `/publications/`, `/news/`                               |

Because multiple labs and companies share the name "Hauser Group", always pair
**Hauser Group** with **Pharmacoinformatics**, **GPCR** and **University of
Copenhagen** in titles, descriptions, READMEs and external profiles.
